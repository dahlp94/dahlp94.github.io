Course Overview

College Algebra develops foundational algebraic skills through equations and inequalities, functions and graphs, systems of equations, and related mathematical structures.

Teaching Focus

In teaching College Algebra, I emphasize understanding relationships between algebraic expressions, equations, functions, and graphs rather than treating them as isolated procedures. The goal is for students to understand both how to carry out a method and how to recognize when it applies.

  • solving and interpreting equations and inequalities;
  • working with functions and their representations;
  • connecting algebraic and graphical reasoning;
  • solving systems of equations;
  • developing algebraic reasoning needed for later quantitative coursework.
