Principles of Statistics

University of Arkansas

Course Overview

Principles of Statistics is a problem-oriented course that introduces statistical reasoning through applications from a range of fields. The course emphasizes probability, statistical patterns, and the use of statistical analysis to make decisions when outcomes are uncertain.

University of Arkansas course catalog →

Teaching Focus

In teaching this course, I emphasize the interpretation of statistical ideas alongside computation. Students should be able to explain what a result means, identify the assumptions behind an analysis, and connect a statistical procedure to the question being asked.

  • understanding data through descriptive summaries and visualization;
  • reasoning about probability and uncertainty;
  • interpreting confidence intervals and hypothesis tests;
  • understanding relationships between variables;
  • communicating statistical conclusions in context.
